# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user requested a static website; no framework).

## Users

Primary: founders and operators at tech startups who need an operating stack for the business without a heavyweight ERP.

## Product Purpose

Tackelabs is building a SaaS "business operating system" — an ERP-lite covering accounting, HR, sales, and inventory for small and medium businesses. The current surface is a coming-soon page for tackelabs.com announcing the product and collecting early-access interest. Success = waitlist signups.

## Positioning

The operational core of a company in one system, sized for businesses that real ERPs ignore — lightweight where ERPs are heavy, unified where startups stitch together five tools.

## Operating Context

Coming-soon marketing surface only; the product itself is not yet public. English-language site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site, no backend. Waitlist form must work without a server (e.g., mailto, formspree-style placeholder, or store-and-show confirmation — undecided endpoint; form must not pretend to submit somewhere real unless configured).
- No pricing, customer, benchmark, or launch-date claims may be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Tackelabs (domain: tackelabs.com).
- Site language: English.

## Evidence on Hand

None — no logo files, testimonials, screenshots, or copy exist yet. All content must be authored; commercial claims must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Sell the unified system, not a feature list.
- Lightweight credibility: calm, competent, honest about being early.
- Every claim on the page must be true today.
